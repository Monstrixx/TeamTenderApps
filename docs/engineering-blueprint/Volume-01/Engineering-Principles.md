# Engineering Principles

Our engineering principles guide our technical decision-making process.

1. **Simplicity over complexity**: Choose the simplest solution that effectively solves the problem.
2. **Design for scale**: Anticipate growth and design systems that can handle increased load gracefully.
3. **Automate everything**: Reduce manual toil through CI/CD, automated testing, and infrastructure as code.
4. **Security by default**: Incorporate security best practices at every stage of the development lifecycle.
5. **Data-driven decisions**: Base technical decisions on metrics, logging, and observability.
