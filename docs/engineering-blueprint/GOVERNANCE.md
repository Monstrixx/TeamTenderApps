# Engineering Governance

This document outlines the governance model for the TeamTender engineering team.

## Decision Making Process

We use ADRs (Architecture Decision Records) and RFCs (Requests for Comments) to drive technical decisions.

- **[ADRs](./ADR/README.md)**: For finalizing and recording architectural decisions.
- **[RFCs](./RFC/README.md)**: For proposing new ideas and gathering feedback before implementation.
