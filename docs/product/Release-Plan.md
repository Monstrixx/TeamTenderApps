## Release Stages

1. **Alpha**
   - **Focus:** Foundational features (Workspace, Company, Personnel).
   - **Audience:** Internal engineering and QA.
   - **Goal:** Validate core architecture and multi-tenancy.

2. **Internal Demo**
   - **Focus:** Equipment and initial Tender workflows.
   - **Audience:** Internal stakeholders and founders.
   - **Goal:** Feedback on UX and core business process flows.

3. **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)**
   - **Focus:** End-to-end Tender lifecycle with basic AI classification.
   - **Audience:** Select design partners and early adopters.
   - **Goal:** Prove product-market fit and validate business value.

4. **Beta**
   - **Focus:** Performance scaling, enhanced AI features, bug fixes.
   - **Audience:** Waitlist customers.
   - **Goal:** Stress test infrastructure and refine features.

5. **Production (General Availability)**
   - **Focus:** Full feature availability, enterprise-grade stability.
   - **Audience:** Open public.
   - **Goal:** Revenue generation and scale.
