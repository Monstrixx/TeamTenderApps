# Blueprint Review Checklist

When reviewing a new addition to the Engineering Blueprint (ADR, RFC, or general document), please verify the following:

- [ ] **Clarity**: Is the document easy to understand?
- [ ] **Completeness**: Are all relevant sections of the template filled out?
- [ ] **Alignment**: Does this align with our [Engineering Principles](../Volume-01/Engineering-Principles.md)?
- [ ] **Impact**: Are the consequences and trade-offs clearly stated?
- [ ] **Formatting**: Does it follow standard Markdown formatting?
