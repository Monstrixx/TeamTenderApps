## Navigation Philosophy
TeamTender prioritizes mental map stability and contextual clarity. Users should always know where they are (Breadcrumbs), what workspace they are operating in (Context Switching), and what tools are available (Primary Navigation).

## Primary Navigation
Located on the left sidebar, the primary navigation surfaces the core domains defined in Wave 6: Workspace Home, Company, Personnel, Equipment, Tender, and AI Center.

## Secondary Navigation
Located as top tabs or indented sidebar menus, secondary navigation allows diving into specific workflows within a domain (e.g., Tender -> Active Tenders, Archives, Drafts).

## Breadcrumb
A persistent trail at the top left of the content area. Example: `Workspace Name / Tender / Bidding / Project Alpha`.

## Context Navigation
Contextual menus that appear when a specific entity is selected (e.g., viewing an Equipment asset exposes a local navigation bar for Asset Details, Maintenance Logs, and Assignments).

## Global Search
Located in the top header, powered by the AI Domain (EPIC-06). Allows instant querying of documents, personnel, equipment, and tenders across the entire workspace.

## Quick Actions
A floating action button (FAB) or a persistent "New" button in the top header, providing rapid access to common tasks: Create Tender, Invite User, Add Equipment.

## Notification Center
Located in the top right header. Alerts users to tender deadlines, AI extraction completions, and role assignment changes.

## Context Switching
A dropdown menu positioned at the very top of the primary sidebar, allowing users who belong to multiple organizations to quickly switch active workspaces without logging out.
