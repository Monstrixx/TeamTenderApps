## Vision for Wave 6
Wave 6 translates our North Star—an AI-driven, engineering-focused B2B SaaS platform—into tangible capabilities. This execution package focuses on laying the fundamental data domains (Workspace, Company, Personnel, Equipment) required to power our advanced Tender management and AI capabilities.

We will deliver a secure, scalable MVP that validates our Enterprise Architecture and demonstrates undeniable customer value through automated, AI-assisted operations.
