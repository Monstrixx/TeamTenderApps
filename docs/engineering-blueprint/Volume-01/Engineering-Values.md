# Engineering Values

Our engineering values define our culture and how we work together as a team.

1. **Collaboration**: We work together, share knowledge, and support each other.
2. **Ownership**: We take responsibility for our code, from design to deployment and operation.
3. **Continuous Learning**: We embrace new technologies and strive to improve our skills constantly.
4. **Transparency**: We communicate openly about our progress, challenges, and failures.
5. **Empathy**: We respect our users and our colleagues, and we build products with them in mind.
