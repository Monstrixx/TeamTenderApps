## Terms & Definitions

- **Workspace:** The secure, isolated digital environment provided to a single customer organization. Acts as the definitive tenant boundary.
- **Tenant:** Interchangeable with Workspace in technical contexts; represents a single subscribing customer entity.
- **Organization:** The real-world business entity that owns the Workspace (represented digitally in the Company module).
- **Project:** A specific undertaking or initiative within a Workspace, often linked directly to a Tender.
- **Tender:** A formal offer or bid submitted in response to an RFP/RFQ, managed entirely within the Workspace.
- **Member:** A human user who has been invited into and authenticated within a Workspace.
- **Role:** A named collection of Permissions assigned to a Member (e.g., Administrator, Manager).
- **Permission:** A discrete, granular right to perform a specific action (e.g., `tender:create`).
- **Capability:** A business function provided by the platform (e.g., "Automated Document Validation").
- **Domain:** A high-level categorization of business logic (e.g., the Personnel Domain, the Equipment Domain).
- **Module:** The UI and API surface area that exposes a Domain to the user.
- **Knowledge:** Institutional memory and data indexed by the AI Center for a specific Workspace.
